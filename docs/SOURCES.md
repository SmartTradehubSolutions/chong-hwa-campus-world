# School references and media provenance

School assets are credited here for traceability. These citations do not grant a reuse license; see [ASSET_RIGHTS.md](../ASSET_RIGHTS.md).

## School and campus photography

- [Official school website](https://www.chonghwakl.edu.my/).
- [Campus facilities directory](https://www.chonghwakl.edu.my/campusfacilities/): building names, facility descriptions and linked panoramas.
- [Media manifest](media-manifest.json): individual panorama source pages, original download URLs and filenames under `public/media/`.
- [Official campus map](https://www.chonghwakl.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/20251224-CampusMap-Pg1.jpg): stored as `public/media/campus-map.jpg`.
- [Sports centre opening article](https://www.chonghwakl.edu.my/2025/02/28/20250228-liankehuodongzhongxinqiyongli-news/): architectural references for the arena. The local `public/media/arena-photo.jpg` is a conventional photograph, not a 360° panorama.

The panoramas currently mapped to building visits retain their original school-source resolution; other preview JPEGs have been resized for web delivery. Files in `public/thumbs/` are derived from these photographs; `arena.jpg` derives from the arena photograph. They remain subject to the source material's rights.

## Authentic branding

- `public/branding/chkl-official-crest.png`: [school site icon](https://www.chonghwakl.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/chklheaderlogoicon.png), 120 × 120 pixels.
- `public/branding/chkl-official-header.png`: [school bilingual header](https://www.chonghwakl.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/chklheaderlogo.png), 753 × 166 pixels.

These supplied PNGs retain their original colors and proportions. The crest is used on the interface and selected scene elements; its presence in the model does not imply that every placement is documented on the physical campus.

## Uniform and building references

- [Official 2024 bookstore guide](https://www.chonghwakl.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2024-BookStoreGuide20241002.pdf).
- [Official illustrated uniform price list](https://www.chonghwakl.edu.my/dev/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/%E6%A0%A1%E6%9C%8D%E4%BB%B7%E7%9B%AE%E8%A1%A8%E9%99%84%E5%9B%BE.pdf).
- [Dataran panorama](https://www.chonghwakl.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/campus360-DataranCHKL.jpg): courtyard materials, basketball colors and adjoining façades.
- [Main entrance panorama](https://www.chonghwakl.edu.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/campus360-MainEntrance.jpg): folding gate, brick piers and entrance context.

Student models interpret the referenced white shirts and shorts for boys and blue pinafores over white blouses for girls, with white socks and shoes. The characters are fictional and simplified. Teacher clothing is illustrative; it is not claimed to be an official teacher uniform.

The model combines references from different dates, including 2020/2023 panoramas and the more recent campus map. Dimensions, relative placement, heights, some façade details and wayfinding signs are approximations. The globe intentionally rearranges buildings for exploration. This app is not an authoritative campus survey, emergency route map or statement of current facility condition.

## Experience inspiration

[CLHS](https://clhs.manfye.com/) inspired the idea of unfolding a campus experience into a map. [Jalan KL](https://jalankl.themasterofnone.xyz/) inspired walkable exploration. This is an acknowledgment of the references, not a claim of shared authorship, affiliation or endorsement.

## Material realism update · 10 September 2026

The reconstructed scene uses locally hosted albedo, OpenGL normal and roughness maps from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/). Seven CC0 material sets are documented in [texture-manifest.json](texture-manifest.json). Metric texture coordinates keep brick and paving size consistent across large façades. These generic surface scans approximate the referenced concrete, brick and roof finishes; they do not establish exact school materials.

Entrance interactions now open the first existing, verified school panorama directly. A viewer selector exposes other mapped views in the same building. No unverified room has been assigned to an otherwise undocumented block. Blocks I, J, G, E and C currently show facility information; L shows an ordinary official photograph. They are not presented as scanned or 360° interiors.
