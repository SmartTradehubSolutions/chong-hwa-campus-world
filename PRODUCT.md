# Chong Hwa Campus World

## Register
Product: an interactive campus explorer for students, families, alumni and visitors.

## Purpose and users
Visitors explore Chong Hwa Independent High School KL, switch between globe, campus and flat map, walk as a student, and open official school panoramas. Keep keyboard and touch access, campus stamps and basketball available.

## Design intent
The user requests a more realistic campus and rejects the existing LEGO-like appearance. Use school references for architecture and authentic branding; add tactile photographic materials and believable daylight without claiming surveyed or scanned geometry. Real photography must remain distinguishable from the reconstructed scene.
